Watch-party display

Changes are shared with every connected TV within five seconds. All displayed times use Eastern Time.

Ticker headlines expire after 30 minutes. Standing summaries are labeled. No lead is presented as a winner call.

Watchlist & full contest roster

Pin races for the TV watchlist and ticker priority. Details list all candidates; county portions are labeled separately from full-district results.

Feed health & coverage

Checked time, source-publication time, and observed changes are separate. Host-imported snapshots are not live feeds. Complete national coverage requires an authorized provider.

Import a sourced snapshot

Use a documented provider export or a manually verified snapshot. Every race needs a stable ID, source link, election date, candidates and counts. Polls need full field dates and methodology. Imports are labeled as host snapshots; they never claim an automatic live connection. See README.md for the schema.

Imports replace the selected source snapshot. They do not add duplicate totals to another provider. Originals are retained in bounded snapshot history.

Election-night checklist

  1. Select live mode and verify Florida’s election date is November 3, 2026.
  2. Check the source-publication timestamps and gaps. Test the county feed and national provider before guests arrive.
  3. Choose your pinned races, readable text size, rotation interval and ticker speed.
  4. Open the TV screen in fullscreen. Test internet loss and recovery.
  5. Keep host controls available. A leading candidate is not a projected or certified winner.
